How this works

The icons up top

Home — back to category selection. Picking a category you already started jumps straight to its results.

Refresh — clears everything. Tap once to arm it, tap again to confirm.

Info — this screen.

Categories

Each category (U14/U17, Girls/Boys) keeps its own separate results — entering U14 Girls doesn't affect U17 Boys, etc.

Entering results

For each category you'll enter your own result, the current best, and a competitor's result, for Slalom, Trick and Jump.

Names

Your name is required. You can also name your competitor(s) — either while entering their result, or afterwards by tapping their name in the results table.

Playing around with numbers

Once everything's entered, tap any value in the "Performances" table to edit it — the Overall numbers update live.
