This website is currently still in a testing phase. The numbers it calculates are usually correct and match the ones used by the IWWF, but they shouldn't be relied upon.

This is a beta feature and only gives an estimate so far, not an exact score. Use the number it gives you as a starting point: put it into the charts above and check how the numbers change. Since those numbers are usually right, you can adjust from there to see how the Overall plays out once the changes are actually entered.

How this works

The icons up top

Home — takes you back to the mode selection screen. Your data is kept.

Overall (medal) — jumps straight to the results tables, using whatever you've entered so far. Anything missing just shows as 0.

↻

Refresh — clears everything and starts over. Tap once to arm it, tap again to confirm.

i

Info — this screen.

Entering results

Pick Slalom, Trick, Jump, or Overall from the home screen, then enter your own result, the current best, and your competitor's result for that event.

Starting speed

This is not your own personal starting speed — it's the minimum starting speed used at the competition.

"Best" result

When it asks for the best score, that means the score of the skier who currently holds 1000 overall points according to the IWWF.

Names

You can name both yourself and your competitor(s) — either while entering their result, or afterwards by tapping their name directly in the results table.

Playing around with numbers

Once everything's entered, tap any value in the "Performances" table to edit it — the Overall numbers update live, so you can try out different scores and see how the standings change.
